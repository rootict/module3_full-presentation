Internet vs. World Wide Web

Understanding the core difference

The Internet

The Infrastructure
  • The physical global network of interconnected computers.
  • Components: Cables, Routers, Servers.Hardware
  • Analogy: The Internet is the Road Network.

The Web (WWW)

The Services
  • Services that run on top of the Internet.
  • Examples: Websites, Email, WhatsApp.Software
  • Analogy: The Web and Emails are the Shops built on that road.

MODULE 3.3 : EVALUATING CONTENT

Information vs. Misinformation

How to identify what you can trust

✅

Reliable Facts

Verified Content
  • Has clear, cited sources and author details.
  • Reported by multiple reputed news agencies.
  • Neutral tone without emotional manipulation.
  • Rule: "Cross-reference before believing."
⚠️

Misinformation

Fake News & Deepfakes
  • Clickbait titles designed to trigger anger or fear.
  • AI-generated images (Deepfakes) or manipulated videos.
  • Shared primarily via untraceable social media chains.
  • Rule: "If it looks too shocking to be true, verify it."

MODULE 3.4 : DIGITAL SAFETY & ETHICS

Emails & Online Communication

Handling your digital interactions responsibly

📥

Handling Emails & Downloads

Inbox Security
  • Always verify the actual Sender Address, not just the display name.
  • Never click links or download `.exe`, `.zip` files from unknown sources.
  • Use Anti-Virus software to scan attachments before opening.
  • Rule: "Think before you click."
🤝

Responsible Communication

Digital Etiquette
  • Maintain a professional tone and use clear Subject Lines.
  • Respect privacy: Do not forward sensitive information or BCC blindly.
  • Be mindful of your digital footprint when engaging in online debates.
  • Rule: "Communicate online as you would in person."
